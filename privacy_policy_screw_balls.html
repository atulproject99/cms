Screw Balls Privacy Policy

Screw it. Just one more level.

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information the Screw Balls mobile game ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. Screw Balls is free to play and is paid for by ads. We collect as little as we can to run the game, show ads and understand how the game is played.

We do not sell your personal information.

Information kept only on your device

  • Your player name. The name you type when you start is saved on your phone and shown in the game. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Game progress. Your level, coins and sound setting are saved on your phone so you can continue where you left off.

Android may include this data in your device's Google backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Information collected and why

Ads and your consent

Screw Balls uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads that give in-game help. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game menu under Privacy options.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Internet connection check

The game needs an internet connection and working ads to be played. To check this, the app briefly connects to Google's servers and ad servers. No personal information is sent as part of this check.

Notifications

The app may ask permission to send notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your Android settings for Screw Balls.

Third-party services

These services handle data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging)
  • Google AdMob and Google Privacy Policy

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Information saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below with the subject "Delete my data". We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Screw Balls is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and collect only what is needed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
